<h2 id="text-boxes"><a class="anchor" href="#text-boxes" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Text boxes</h2><p>While you type in a text box (a subtitle line, the project name, a post), the usual text keys work: copy, cut, paste, select all, and undo or redo <strong>inside that box</strong>. The editor&#39;s single-key shortcuts (Space, S, Delete, the arrows) are switched off while you type, so they never interrupt your writing. Click outside the box, or press Tab, to use them again.</p>
<p>Editor shortcuts also pause while a menu or a dialog is open, and while you&#39;re on the Settings or Voices page.</p>
