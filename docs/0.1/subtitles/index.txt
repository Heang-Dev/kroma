2b:T2a20,<p>Before Kroma can translate, it needs the original lines: what&#39;s said, and when. This page covers getting those lines, fixing them in the <strong>Subtitles</strong> list, and timing them on the timeline.</p>
<h2 id="where-the-lines-come-from"><a class="anchor" href="#where-the-lines-come-from" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Where the lines come from</h2><p>Kroma has two ways to get the original lines.</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Way</th>
<th>When it&#39;s used</th>
<th>Good to know</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><strong>The site&#39;s subtitles</strong></td>
<td>The video was downloaded with the site&#39;s own Chinese subtitles</td>
<td>Instant, and usually exact. No key needed.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Speech to text (cloud)</strong></td>
<td>Every other time</td>
<td>Listens to the video. Needs a key, see <a href="/kroma/docs/0.1/configuration/">Add your keys</a>.</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>When you press <strong>Make Khmer subtitles</strong> in <strong>Translate</strong>, Kroma picks for you: the site&#39;s Chinese subtitles if the video has them, speech-to-text if not. Then it translates.</p>
<p>To run just one part, use the <strong>Step by step</strong> section of <strong>Translate</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Use the site&#39;s subtitles</strong>: shows only when the video has the site&#39;s Chinese subtitles.</li>
<li><strong>Speech to text (cloud)</strong>: listens to the video, even when site subtitles exist.</li>
</ul>
<p>Either one <strong>replaces</strong> the lines of that video. When it&#39;s done you&#39;ll see a message like <strong>312 subtitle lines ready.</strong> If nothing was heard, it says <strong>No speech was found.</strong></p>
<h2 id="speech-to-text"><a class="anchor" href="#speech-to-text" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Speech to text</h2><h3 id="spoken-language"><a class="anchor" href="#spoken-language" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Spoken language</h3><p>In <strong>Translate</strong>, set <strong>Spoken language</strong> before you start: <strong>Chinese (Mandarin)</strong> (the default), <strong>Cantonese</strong>, <strong>English</strong>, <strong>Korean</strong>, <strong>Japanese</strong>, <strong>Thai</strong>, <strong>Vietnamese</strong>, or <strong>Detect automatically</strong>. Choosing the right one gives better results than letting it guess.</p>
<h3 id="services"><a class="anchor" href="#services" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Services</h3><p>Choose the service in <strong>Settings → Speech to Text</strong>:</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Service</th>
<th>Cost</th>
<th>Speed</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><strong>Gemini</strong> (default)</td>
<td>Uses your Gemini key from <strong>Settings → Translation</strong>, nothing else to set up</td>
<td>Very accurate, with punctuation. About a minute for every 2 minutes of speech.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Groq</strong></td>
<td>Free key</td>
<td>The fastest: Whisper large-v3, a whole movie in about a minute.</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td>Paid</td>
<td>Whisper.</td>
</tr>

<p>There&#39;s also a custom option under <strong>Advanced</strong> for any OpenAI-compatible service (an <strong>Endpoint</strong> and a <strong>Model</strong>).</p>
<h3 id="how-it-works"><a class="anchor" href="#how-it-works" aria-hidden="true">#</a>How it works</h3><p>Kroma cuts the sound into pieces (2 minutes each for Gemini, 10 minutes for Whisper) and sends several at once. You&#39;ll see <code>Transcribing with Gemini · 3/12 parts</code>. How many go at once is <strong>Parallel requests</strong> in <strong>Settings → Translation</strong> (2, 4, 6 or 8; 4 by default). Lower it if a service says you&#39;re sending too much.</p>
<p>Kroma also tidies the result:</p>
<ul>
<li>Long lines are split at their punctuation, so each line is one readable sentence (no more than about 7 seconds).</li>
<li>Lines that speech-to-text often invents over music or silence in Chinese videos (like &quot;thanks for watching&quot;) are dropped.</li>
<li>A line repeated by mistake is removed.</li>
</ul>
<p>If Gemini&#39;s main model is busy or its free daily limit is used up, Kroma uses the lighter <strong>Flash-Lite</strong> model for those parts. You&#39;ll see <strong>lines ready, lower accuracy</strong>. For full accuracy on long videos, add a free Groq key.</p>
<h2 id="the-subtitles-list"><a class="anchor" href="#the-subtitles-list" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The Subtitles list</h2><p>The <strong>Subtitles</strong> panel on the right lists every line. Its header shows the count, like <strong>312 lines · 298 in Khmer</strong>.</p>
<p>Each line shows:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>The time</strong>, like <code>00:12.40 → 00:15.00</code>. Click it to jump the video to that line.</li>
<li><strong>The original line</strong>, in grey. You can select and copy it.</li>
<li><strong>The translation</strong>, below it. Click and type to change it. An empty one says <strong>Not translated yet</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Who says it</strong>, a coloured dot with a name, once the video has characters (see below).</li>
<li><strong>Delete line</strong>: the bin icon that appears when you point at the line.</li>
</ul>
<p>While the video plays, the current line is highlighted and the list scrolls to keep it in view. Clicking a line on the timeline scrolls to it here too.</p>

<td><strong>Find and replace</strong></td>
<td>Changes a word in every translated line at once. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.1/translation/#find-and-replace">Translation</a>.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Add a line at the playhead</strong></td>
<td>Adds an empty 2-second line where the playhead is. Type the text into it.</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<h3 id="find-in-subtitles"><a class="anchor" href="#find-in-subtitles" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Find in subtitles</h3><p>The <strong>Find in subtitles</strong> box above the list hides every line that doesn&#39;t contain your words. It searches both the original and the translation. Clear the box to see everything again.</p>
<h3 id="speaker-colours"><a class="anchor" href="#speaker-colours" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Speaker colours</h3><p>After you find the characters in <strong>Voice</strong> (see <a href="/kroma/docs/0.1/characters-and-voices/">Characters &amp; voices</a>), each line shows who says it, with that character&#39;s colour. Click the name to choose another character, <strong>Default voice</strong>, or <strong>New character…</strong>.</p>
<h3 id="undo"><a class="anchor" href="#undo" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Undo</h3><p>Made a mistake? Press <strong>Undo</strong> at the top (<code>⌘Z</code>), and <strong>Redo</strong> (<code>⇧⌘Z</code>) to bring it back.</p>
<h2 id="the-timeline"><a class="anchor" href="#the-timeline" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The timeline</h2><p>The timeline at the bottom has two tracks:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Subtitles</strong>: one block per line. A coloured block is translated; a grey block isn&#39;t yet.</li>
<li><strong>Video</strong>: your clips, with small pictures and the sound&#39;s waveform.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="moving-around"><a class="anchor" href="#moving-around" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Moving around</h3><ul>
<li>Click the time ruler at the top to move the playhead there, or drag the playhead&#39;s handle.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Space</strong> to play or pause.</li>
<li>Press <code>←</code> or <code>→</code> to step one frame, or hold <strong>Shift</strong> for 5 seconds.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="zoom"><a class="anchor" href="#zoom" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Zoom</h3><p>Use <strong>Zoom out</strong>, the zoom slider and <strong>Zoom in</strong> at the top right of the timeline. <strong>Fit the whole video</strong> shows everything at once. You can also pinch on a trackpad, or hold <code>⌘</code> and scroll, to zoom around the pointer.</p>
<h3 id="timing-a-line"><a class="anchor" href="#timing-a-line" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Timing a line</h3><p>Click a subtitle block to select it. The bar above says <em>Subtitle selected · drag to move, edges to retime</em>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Drag the middle of a block to move the line earlier or later.</li>
<li>Drag its left or right edge to change when it starts or ends. A line is never shorter than 0.2 seconds.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="cutting-the-video"><a class="anchor" href="#cutting-the-video" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Cutting the video</h3><p>Click a clip to select it (<em>Video clip selected · drag to reorder, edges to trim</em>).</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Split at the playhead</strong> (the scissors, or press <code>S</code>) cuts the clip in two where the playhead is.</li>
<li><strong>Delete the selection</strong> (the bin, or <code>Delete</code>) removes the selected clip, subtitle line or object.</li>
<li>Drag a clip to change its place. A line shows where it will land.</li>
<li>Drag a clip&#39;s edges to trim its start or end.</li>
</ul>
<p>The subtitles follow the video. When you cut out a part, the lines in that part go and the lines after it move up to stay in sync.</p>
<p>Drag the top edge of the timeline to make it taller or shorter.</p>
<h2 id="more-than-one-clip"><a class="anchor" href="#more-than-one-clip" aria-hidden="true">#</a>More than one clip</h2><p>Speech-to-text and the site&#39;s subtitles work on the video of the <strong>first clip</strong> on the timeline. To get lines for another video:</p>
<ol>
<li>Drag its clip to the start of the timeline.</li>
<li>In <strong>Translate → Step by step</strong>, press <strong>Speech to text (cloud)</strong>. Only that video&#39;s lines are replaced.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Translate missing</strong>, so the lines you already fixed keep your changes.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Make Khmer subtitles</strong> and <strong>Translate all</strong> translate every line again, which replaces any hand fixes.</p>
<h2 id="saving-subtitle-files"><a class="anchor" href="#saving-subtitle-files" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Saving subtitle files</h2><p>To save the lines as <code>.srt</code> files (for YouTube&#39;s subtitle upload, or to keep a copy), open <strong>Post</strong> and use <strong>Subtitle files</strong>: <strong>Khmer .srt</strong> or <strong>Original .srt</strong>. They go into your export folder. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.1/posting/">Posting</a>.</p>
