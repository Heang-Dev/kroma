32:T3f16,<p>Kroma can post your finished video to YouTube, Facebook and TikTok in one click. The AI writes a title, text and hashtags for each platform, and you can edit them before posting.</p>
<p>Only post videos you have the right to use. If you translated someone else&#39;s video, make sure you have their permission to share it.</p>
<h2 id="how-it-works"><a class="anchor" href="#how-it-works" aria-hidden="true">#</a>How it works</h2><p>Kroma doesn&#39;t post to the platforms directly. It posts through a service called <strong>Upload-Post</strong> (upload-post.com). YouTube, Facebook and TikTok have already approved Upload-Post, so your posts go out publicly.</p>
<p>You set this up once:</p>
<ol>
<li>Make an Upload-Post account and an API key.</li>
<li>Paste the key into Kroma.</li>
<li>Connect your YouTube, Facebook and TikTok accounts.</li>
</ol>
<p>After that, posting is one click from the <strong>Post</strong> panel.</p>

<p>The free plan allows <strong>10 posts a month</strong>. When they&#39;re used up, you&#39;ll see &quot;This month&#39;s Upload-Post uploads are used up (the free plan has 10). Upgrade the plan or wait for next month.&quot;</p>
<p>These are Upload-Post&#39;s plans, not Kroma&#39;s. Kroma itself is free.</p>
<h2 id="set-up-posting-once"><a class="anchor" href="#set-up-posting-once" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Set up posting (once)</h2><h3 id="1-make-an-account-and-an-api-key"><a class="anchor" href="#1-make-an-account-and-an-api-key" aria-hidden="true">#</a>1. Make an account and an API key</h3><p>An API key is a long password that lets Kroma use your Upload-Post account.</p>
<ol>
<li>Open <strong>Settings</strong> (⌘,) → <strong>Posting</strong>. Under <strong>Getting started</strong>, press <strong>Open</strong> to go to upload-post.com, and sign up for free.</li>
<li>In Upload-Post, go to <strong>API Keys</strong> and create a key. Copy it.</li>
<li>Back in Kroma, paste it into <strong>API key</strong> and save it.</li>
</ol>
<p>The <strong>Account</strong> row now shows your email and plan, for example &quot;<a href="mailto:you@example.com">you@example.com</a> · free plan&quot;, with a green tick. Press <strong>Check</strong> to check again. If the key is wrong, you&#39;ll see &quot;Upload-Post refused the API key.&quot;</p>
<h3 id="2-connect-your-accounts"><a class="anchor" href="#2-connect-your-accounts" aria-hidden="true">#</a>2. Connect your accounts</h3><ol>
<li>Under <strong>Your accounts</strong>, press <strong>Connect</strong>.</li>
<li>A secure Upload-Post page opens in your browser. Sign in to YouTube, Facebook and TikTok, and allow access. (The page works for 48 hours.)</li>
<li>Come back to Kroma. It refreshes by itself when you return.</li>
</ol>
<p>Each platform now shows the connected account name, or <strong>Not connected</strong>. If a sign-in has run out, it says &quot;sign-in expired: press Connect&quot;. Press <strong>Connect</strong> again to fix it.</p>
<p>Upload-Post keeps your accounts in a <strong>profile</strong>. If you don&#39;t have one yet, Kroma makes one called &quot;Kroma Studio&quot; for you. If your Upload-Post account has more than one profile, a <strong>Profile</strong> menu lets you choose which one Kroma posts to.</p>
<h3 id="3-choose-your-facebook-page"><a class="anchor" href="#3-choose-your-facebook-page" aria-hidden="true">#</a>3. Choose your Facebook Page</h3><p>Facebook only lets apps post to <strong>Pages</strong>, not personal profiles. After you connect Facebook, pick your Page in the <strong>Facebook Page</strong> menu. If it says <strong>No Pages found</strong>, make a Page on Facebook first.</p>
<p>If you have exactly one Page, Kroma uses it automatically.</p>
<h2 id="post-a-video"><a class="anchor" href="#post-a-video" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Post a video</h2><p>Open the <strong>Post</strong> panel (left menu). From top to bottom: <strong>1 · Posts</strong>, <strong>2 · Post to</strong>, <strong>Posted</strong> (once you&#39;ve posted), <strong>Cover</strong> and <strong>Subtitle files</strong>.</p>
<h3 id="1-posts"><a class="anchor" href="#1-posts" aria-hidden="true">#</a>1 · Posts</h3><p>Press <strong>Write the posts</strong>. The AI reads your translated subtitles and writes one post for each platform, in the subtitles&#39; language:</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Platform</th>
<th>What the AI writes</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><strong>YouTube</strong></td>
<td>A title people can search for (under 90 characters) and a description of 2–3 short paragraphs</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Facebook</strong></td>
<td>A warm post that makes people curious</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>TikTok</strong></td>
<td>A very short hook</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>Each post also gets 5–10 hashtags, mixing your language and English. The AI is told not to make up facts that aren&#39;t in the video. It also uses the project&#39;s name and your translation notes.</p>
<p>Each post is a card with a <strong>title</strong>, the <strong>text</strong> and the <strong>hashtags</strong>. Edit anything:</p>
<ul>
<li>Type in the title and text boxes.</li>
<li>Type hashtags with or without <code>#</code>, separated by spaces or commas, for example <code>#food #fyp</code>. They&#39;re saved when you leave the box or press Enter.</li>
<li><strong>Copy</strong> copies the whole post (title, text and hashtags), if you want to paste it somewhere yourself.</li>
</ul>
<p>Your posts are saved with the project. Press <strong>Write them again</strong> for new ones.</p>
<p>Writing posts needs an AI key: Gemini (free) or Claude. Google Translate can&#39;t write posts. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/configuration/">Add your keys</a>. Without one you&#39;ll see &quot;Writing posts needs an AI: add a free Gemini key (or a Claude key) in Settings → Translation.&quot;</p>
<p>If you press <strong>Post</strong> before writing the posts, Kroma writes them first.</p>
<h3 id="2-post-to"><a class="anchor" href="#2-post-to" aria-hidden="true">#</a>2 · Post to</h3><p>If you haven&#39;t set up posting, this section shows a <strong>Connect accounts</strong> button that opens <strong>Settings → Posting</strong>.</p>
<p>Otherwise:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Tick the platforms.</strong> Connected accounts are ticked to start with. A platform that isn&#39;t connected is greyed out and says <strong>Not connected</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Choose each platform&#39;s format</strong>, on the right of its row: <strong>16:9</strong> or <strong>9:16</strong>. YouTube starts on <strong>16:9</strong>, Facebook and TikTok on <strong>9:16</strong>. Each platform gets the file in its own format. See <a href="#formats-per-platform">Formats per platform</a>.</li>
<li><strong>YouTube visibility:</strong> <strong>Public</strong> (the default), <strong>Unlisted</strong> (only people with the link can see it) or <strong>Private</strong> (only you).</li>
<li><strong>TikTok:</strong> <strong>Post now</strong> posts straight away. <strong>To drafts</strong> sends it to your TikTok drafts, so you can add music or effects in the TikTok app, then post it yourself.</li>
<li><strong>Video</strong> (or <strong>Videos</strong>, when the platforms need both formats): if you exported a format and nothing has changed since, Kroma reuses that file (<em>Uses your 16:9 export (nothing changed since).</em>). Otherwise it says what it will export, and you choose the <strong>Export size</strong>: <strong>Small</strong>, <strong>Standard</strong> (the default) or <strong>High</strong>. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/export/#sizes">Export</a>.</li>
<li>Press the button. It names the platforms, for example <strong>Post to YouTube, Facebook, TikTok</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>The button is greyed out when the timeline is empty, when there are no translated subtitles, or when no platform is ticked (it then says <strong>Choose where to post</strong>).</p>
<h3 id="formats-per-platform"><a class="anchor" href="#formats-per-platform" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Formats per platform</h3><p>YouTube likes wide videos; TikTok, Facebook Reels and Instagram Reels like tall ones. Kroma exports the same project in each format the platforms need, and uploads the right file to each:</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Platform</th>
<th>Starts on</th>
<th>Gets</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>YouTube</td>
<td><strong>16:9</strong></td>
<td>A 1920 × 1080 video</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Facebook</td>
<td><strong>9:16</strong></td>
<td>A 1080 × 1920 Reel (if it&#39;s 90 seconds or shorter)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>TikTok</td>
<td><strong>9:16</strong></td>
<td>A 1080 × 1920 video</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>A wide video in the 9:16 format fills the empty space the way <strong>Style → When it doesn&#39;t fit</strong> says (a blurred copy, by default), and the subtitles are placed for that shape. Change a platform&#39;s format with its switch; your choice applies to this post.</p>
<p>The same choice is on every video in <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/batch/#ready-to-post">Batch → Ready to post</a>.</p>
<h3 id="what-happens-next"><a class="anchor" href="#what-happens-next" aria-hidden="true">#</a>What happens next</h3><ol>
<li>Kroma exports each format the platforms need, unless it can reuse an export.</li>
<li>It uploads each file <strong>once</strong> to Upload-Post, for the platforms that get it, with each platform&#39;s own title and text.</li>
<li>It follows each platform until it&#39;s done. The progress shows, for example, &quot;Posting · 1/3 platforms done&quot;.</li>
</ol>
<p>Platforms can take a while to process a video. Kroma keeps checking for up to 30 minutes. If a platform still isn&#39;t done, it says &quot;Still processing on the platform. Check it there in a few minutes.&quot;</p>
<p>When it finishes, a message says &quot;Posted to YouTube, Facebook&quot;, or &quot;Not posted to TikTok&quot; with the reason. If you sent TikTok to drafts, it reminds you to open the TikTok app and post it from your drafts.</p>
<p>Each post is sent with its own unique key, so if the same upload request reaches Upload-Post twice, it&#39;s posted only once.</p>
<h3 id="what-goes-where"><a class="anchor" href="#what-goes-where" aria-hidden="true">#</a>What goes where</h3><ul>
<li><strong>YouTube:</strong> your title (up to 100 characters), and the text with the hashtags added at the end (up to 5000 characters). The hashtags are also added as YouTube tags.</li>
<li><strong>Facebook:</strong> a <strong>Reel</strong> if its format is <strong>9:16</strong> and the video is 90 seconds or shorter. Otherwise a normal video on your Page. The hashtags are added at the end of the text.</li>
<li><strong>TikTok:</strong> the title and text together, with the hashtags at the end, up to TikTok&#39;s 2200 characters.</li>
</ul>
<p>Anything longer than a platform allows is cut short and ends with &quot;…&quot;.</p>
<h3 id="posted"><a class="anchor" href="#posted" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Posted</h3><p>After you post, a <strong>Posted</strong> section keeps a history for this project, newest first. Each entry shows the date and time, then each platform:</p>
<ul>
<li>a green tick when it&#39;s posted, with an <strong>Open</strong> button that opens the post in your browser,</li>
<li>a red cross when it failed, with the reason,</li>
<li>a spinner while it&#39;s still processing.</li>
</ul>
<p>The history is saved with the project.</p>
<h3 id="episode-covers"><a class="anchor" href="#episode-covers" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Episode covers</h3><p>A good thumbnail gets a video clicked. <strong>Cover</strong>, near the bottom of the <strong>Post</strong> panel, makes one from a frame of the video, with the episode number and the series title on it.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Cover size</strong>: <strong>16:9 · YouTube</strong> (1280 × 720) or <strong>9:16 · TikTok</strong> (1080 × 1920). This only changes the preview: both sizes are saved.</li>
<li><strong>The frame</strong>: Kroma picks a sharp, bright moment without subtitles the first time. Press <strong>Auto-pick a frame</strong> to pick again, or move the playhead and press <strong>Use the playhead</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Design</strong>: <strong>Badge</strong> (a bright episode badge in the corner, the title along the bottom), <strong>Big number</strong> (a huge episode number on the side), <strong>Banner</strong> (a coloured band across the bottom with the title and episode) or <strong>Minimal</strong> (just the episode, small and clean). Each tile shows your frame.</li>
<li><strong>Episode</strong>: read from the project&#39;s name, like <code>05 - …</code>, <code>EP12</code>, <code>Episode 3</code>, <code>第27集</code>, <code>ភាគ ១៤</code>, <code>Part 2</code> or <code>#4</code>. If there&#39;s no number in the name, type one.</li>
<li><strong>Label</strong>: <strong>ភាគ ៥</strong>, <strong>EP 5</strong>, <strong>Part 5</strong>, or <strong>My own word</strong> (type it, like <code>វគ្គ</code> or <code>Episode</code>; a Khmer word gets Khmer digits).</li>
<li><strong>Title</strong>: the series folder&#39;s name, unless you type one.</li>
<li><strong>Colour</strong>: one of the swatches, or <strong>Another colour</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>Press <strong>Save covers</strong>. Both sizes are saved as JPEG next to this episode&#39;s exported videos, named like the export with <code> cover</code> at the end: <code>05 - Title (16x9) cover.jpg</code> and <code>05 - Title (9x16) cover.jpg</code>. A project that isn&#39;t saved to a file puts them in the export folder. Buttons under it show each saved cover in its folder.</p>
<p>If you hid the original subtitles with <strong>Objects → Hide original subtitles</strong>, they stay blurred on the cover too. The cover settings are saved with the project.</p>
<p>Covers aren&#39;t uploaded with the post. Add them yourself on each platform (YouTube: <strong>Thumbnail</strong> in YouTube Studio; TikTok and Reels: choose the cover in the app). To make covers for a whole series at once, turn on <strong>Make covers</strong> in <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/batch/">Batch</a>.</p>
<h3 id="subtitle-files"><a class="anchor" href="#subtitle-files" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Subtitle files</h3><p>At the bottom, <strong>Subtitle files</strong> saves your subtitles as <code>.srt</code> files, for YouTube&#39;s subtitle upload or to keep a copy:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Khmer .srt</strong> (or your language&#39;s name): the translated subtitles.</li>
<li><strong>Original .srt</strong>: the original subtitles.</li>
</ul>
