2b:T1f04,<p>Cloning makes a new voice from a short recording of one person. Once it&#39;s made, you can use it as the project&#39;s voice or give it to any character.</p>
<p>Cloning needs the <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/voice-engine/">voice engine</a> to be running. Cloned voices are made for Khmer.</p>
<h2 id="consent-comes-first"><a class="anchor" href="#consent-comes-first" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Consent comes first</h2><p>Only clone <strong>your own voice</strong>, or the voice of someone who has given you permission.</p>
<p>Before Kroma clones anything, you must tick this box:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>This is my voice, or I have the speaker&#39;s permission to clone it. I won&#39;t use it to pretend to be someone or to mislead people.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>The <strong>Clone voice</strong> button stays greyed out until it&#39;s ticked.</p>
<p>Never use a cloned voice to pretend to be someone else, including singers, actors and other public figures. Don&#39;t clone a voice from a video just because you downloaded it.</p>
<h2 id="where-to-start"><a class="anchor" href="#where-to-start" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Where to start</h2><p>You can open the clone window from two places:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Voices</strong> (left menu) → <strong>Clone voice</strong> at the top right. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/voice-library/">Voice library</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Voice</strong> panel → <strong>Voice type: Cloned</strong> → <strong>Clone a voice</strong> at the bottom of the list. If you have no cloned voices yet, choosing <strong>Cloned</strong> opens the window straight away.</li>
</ul>
<p>Both open the same window, called <strong>Clone a voice</strong>. It has:</p>
<ol>
<li>a <strong>Name</strong> box (for example &quot;My voice&quot;),</li>
<li>a choice between <strong>Record now</strong> and <strong>Use a file</strong>,</li>
<li>the consent box,</li>
<li><strong>Cancel</strong> and <strong>Clone voice</strong> buttons.</li>
</ol>
<p>If you leave the name empty, the voice is called &quot;My voice&quot;.</p>
<h2 id="record-in-the-app"><a class="anchor" href="#record-in-the-app" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Record in the app</h2><p><strong>Record now</strong> is the easiest way, and usually the best.</p>
<ol>
<li>Read the Khmer text in the grey box. It says &quot;Read this aloud, clearly and at an easy pace&quot;. If you&#39;d like a different text, press <strong>Another text</strong> (there are three to choose from).</li>
<li>Press the big red microphone button and start reading.</li>
<li>Bars move as you speak, so you can see the microphone hears you. The counter shows the seconds, for example &quot;8 / 20 s&quot;.</li>
<li>When you finish the text, press the stop button (the red square).</li>
<li>Listen back with the player. Press <strong>Record again</strong> if it isn&#39;t clear.</li>
<li>Type a name, tick the consent box and press <strong>Clone voice</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="how-long"><a class="anchor" href="#how-long" aria-hidden="true">#</a>How long</h3><ul>
<li>At least <strong>5 seconds</strong>. If you stop sooner, you&#39;ll see &quot;That was only 3.2 s. Read the whole text: at least 5 seconds.&quot;</li>
<li>At most <strong>20 seconds</strong>. Recording stops by itself at 20 seconds.</li>
<li>Each text takes about 15 seconds to read at an easy pace.</li>
</ul>
<p>While you record, the hint says <strong>keep reading</strong> for the first 5 seconds, then <strong>press stop when you finish the text</strong>.</p>
<h3 id="why-reading-a-text-helps"><a class="anchor" href="#why-reading-a-text-helps" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Why reading a text helps</h3><p>Because you read a text Kroma already knows, the engine doesn&#39;t need to work out what you said. It skips that step and stores the text exactly as you read it, so there&#39;s no risk of it mishearing the words.</p>
<h3 id="tips-for-a-good-recording"><a class="anchor" href="#tips-for-a-good-recording" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Tips for a good recording</h3><ul>
<li>Record in a quiet room, with no music, TV or fan noise.</li>
<li>Avoid rooms with an echo.</li>
<li>Speak in your normal voice, at an easy pace. The voice will sound the way you read.</li>
<li>When you listen back, check it&#39;s clear, with no music or echo. The app reminds you of this too.</li>
<li>The line under the bars shows which microphone is being used (&quot;Microphone: …&quot;). If it&#39;s the wrong one, change it in your computer&#39;s sound settings.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="microphone-permission-mac"><a class="anchor" href="#microphone-permission-mac" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Microphone permission (Mac)</h3><p>The first time, macOS asks if Kroma may use the microphone. Click <strong>Allow</strong>.</p>
<p>If you said no before, you&#39;ll see &quot;Kroma isn&#39;t allowed to use the microphone.&quot; Press <strong>Open Microphone settings</strong>, turn Kroma on under <strong>Privacy &amp; Security → Microphone</strong>, then try again.</p>
<h2 id="use-a-file"><a class="anchor" href="#use-a-file" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Use a file</h2><p>Choose <strong>Use a file</strong> if you already have a recording.</p>
<ol>
<li>Press <strong>Choose an audio or video file…</strong>.</li>
<li>Pick a file. Kroma accepts WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC, MP4, MOV and WebM.</li>
<li>Type a name, tick the consent box and press <strong>Clone voice</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>For a good clone, the file should have:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>5 to 20 seconds</strong> of speech,</li>
<li><strong>one person</strong> only,</li>
<li>speaking <strong>clearly in Khmer</strong>,</li>
<li><strong>no music</strong> and as little background noise as possible.</li>
</ul>
<p>The engine cleans the recording: it trims it, evens out the volume and keeps at most 20 seconds. With a file, the engine also writes down what is said, because Kroma doesn&#39;t know the words.</p>
<h2 id="what-happens-when-you-press-clone-voice"><a class="anchor" href="#what-happens-when-you-press-clone-voice" aria-hidden="true">#</a>What happens when you press Clone voice</h2><p>The button changes to <strong>Cloning…</strong> and the progress shows the engine&#39;s messages. When it&#39;s done:</p>
<ul>
<li>a message says, for example, &quot;Voice &quot;My voice&quot; is ready.&quot;,</li>
<li>the new voice appears under <strong>Your voices</strong> on the Voices page,</li>
<li>if you started from the <strong>Voice</strong> panel, the new voice becomes the project&#39;s voice.</li>
</ul>
<p>The cloned voice is kept on your computer, in the <code>Kroma Studio/voices</code> folder inside your Movies folder. The engine doesn&#39;t keep a copy.</p>
<h2 id="using-a-cloned-voice"><a class="anchor" href="#using-a-cloned-voice" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Using a cloned voice</h2><ul>
<li><strong>For the whole project:</strong> in the <strong>Voice</strong> panel, choose <strong>Voice type: Cloned</strong> and click the voice. It shows &quot;· in use&quot;. Or, on the Voices page, use the card&#39;s <strong>⋯</strong> menu → <strong>Use in this project</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>For one character:</strong> pick it from the character&#39;s voice menu, under <strong>Your voices</strong>. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/characters-and-voices/">Characters &amp; voices</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>In the <strong>Voice</strong> panel each cloned voice shows its sample length and the words it says. Press ▶ to hear it.</p>
<p><strong>Find automatically</strong> never picks a cloned voice for a character. You always choose it yourself.</p>
<h2 id="deleting-a-cloned-voice"><a class="anchor" href="#deleting-a-cloned-voice" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Deleting a cloned voice</h2><p>Press the bin icon next to the voice in the <strong>Voice</strong> panel, or use <strong>⋯ → Delete</strong> on the Voices page. Kroma asks you to confirm. When you delete it from the <strong>Voice</strong> panel while the project uses it, the project switches back to the first instant voice.</p>
