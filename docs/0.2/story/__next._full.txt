2b:T1887,<p><strong>Story</strong> turns a written script into an audio story with a different voice for every character: a grandfather telling a folk tale, a narrator and two children, a short radio drama. Open it with <strong>Story</strong> in the left menu.</p>
<p>Story uses Kroma&#39;s <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/voice-engine/">voice engine</a>, so start the engine first. The engine&#39;s status shows at the top of the page and in the status bar, with <strong>Start</strong> when it&#39;s off.</p>
<h2 id="the-page"><a class="anchor" href="#the-page" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The page</h2><ul>
<li><strong>On the left</strong>, your stories, newest first, and <strong>New story</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>At the top</strong>, <strong>1 · Write</strong> and <strong>2 · Listen</strong>, the engine&#39;s status, and <strong>Generate story</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Write</strong> has the story&#39;s title, the script, and the <strong>Cast</strong> beside it.</li>
<li><strong>Listen</strong> plays the finished story, with every line listed under the player.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="writing-a-script"><a class="anchor" href="#writing-a-script" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Writing a script</h2><p>Write one line per speaker, with the speaker&#39;s name, a colon, then what they say:</p>
<div class="code-block"><pre><code>Narrator: កាលពីព្រេងនាយ មានក្មេងប្រុសម្នាក់ឈ្មោះសុខា។
[pause 1s]
Sokha (excited): លោកតា! មើលនេះ!
លោកតា៖ យឺតៗ ចៅ។</code></pre></div><div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>You write</th>
<th>What it does</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><code>Sokha: …</code> or <code>សុខា៖ …</code></td>
<td>A line for Sokha. The colon can be <code>:</code>, <code>：</code> or the Khmer <code>៖</code>.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>A line with no name</td>
<td>It&#39;s the <strong>Narrator</strong>&#39;s.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>Sokha (whisper): …</code></td>
<td>One line in its own style (whisper, excited, sad…).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>[pause 2s]</code> on its own line</td>
<td>Silence between lines. <code>[pause 500ms]</code> works too; a bare <code>[pause]</code> is 1 second. Pauses are kept between 0.1 and 10 seconds.</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td>A pause between that line&#39;s words.</td>
</tr>

<p>A name can be up to 30 characters, without <code>:</code> or brackets. The <strong>Insert</strong> bar above the script adds a <strong>Pause</strong>, or a speaker&#39;s name at the start of a new line.</p>
<p><strong>How it will be read</strong>, under the cast, shows the lines the way the engine will read them, as you type.</p>
<h2 id="the-cast"><a class="anchor" href="#the-cast" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The cast</h2><p>Every character in the script shows in <strong>Cast</strong> as soon as you start their line. For each one:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Voice</strong>: the engine&#39;s default voice, one of <strong>Your voices</strong> (cloned), or a preset voice, grouped like the <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/voice-library/">voice library</a>. Kroma picks a fitting one from the name: a &quot;Narrator&quot; gets <em>Narrator (deep male)</em>, &quot;grandpa&quot; or &quot;លោកតា&quot; gets <em>Storyteller (warm grandfather)</em>, &quot;girl&quot; or &quot;ស្រី&quot; gets <em>Young girl</em>, and so on. Change it any time. Instant voices aren&#39;t offered here: stories are read by the engine.</li>
<li><strong>Style</strong> (optional): how this character speaks in every line, like &quot;calm&quot;. A style written in a line, like <code>Sokha (whisper):</code>, is used for that one line.</li>
</ul>
<p>To add a character, type a name under the cast and press <strong>Add</strong>. A new line starts for them in the script, with the cursor ready.</p>
<p>Up to <strong>6</strong> characters get their own voice. More than that read with the Narrator&#39;s voice, and Kroma warns you.</p>
<h2 id="generating-the-story"><a class="anchor" href="#generating-the-story" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Generating the story</h2><p>Give the story a title (or leave <strong>Untitled story</strong>) and press <strong>Generate story</strong>. A progress bar shows while the engine works; it can take a while for a long story, especially on a computer without a fast graphics card. When it&#39;s done you get a notification, <strong>Story ready: …</strong>, and the story opens in <strong>Listen</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="listening-and-fixing-a-line"><a class="anchor" href="#listening-and-fixing-a-line" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Listening and fixing a line</h2><p><strong>Listen</strong> shows the title, how many lines it has, its length and when it was made, then the player. Every line is listed under it, with who says it.</p>
<p>Not happy with one line? Press <strong>↻</strong> next to it. Kroma speaks just that line again and keeps the rest the same. The result is saved as a new story, so the earlier version is still in your list.</p>

<h2 id="where-stories-are-kept"><a class="anchor" href="#where-stories-are-kept" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Where stories are kept</h2><p>Each story has its own folder in <code>stories</code> in the Kroma Studio library folder, with <code>story.mp3</code> (or <code>story.wav</code>), <code>story.srt</code> and <code>story.json</code>. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/projects-and-files/#where-files-live">Projects and files</a>.</p>
