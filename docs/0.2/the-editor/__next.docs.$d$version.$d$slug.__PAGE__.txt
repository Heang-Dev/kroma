<p>When you first open Kroma with no video, the preview says <strong>សូមស្វាគមន៍</strong> (welcome) and tells you to paste a video link or import a file on the left, then press <strong>Make Khmer subtitles</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="the-title-bar"><a class="anchor" href="#the-title-bar" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The title bar</h2><p>From left to right:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Kroma</strong>, then the <strong>project name</strong>. Click the name to rename the project. A small grey dot after it means there are changes not saved to a file yet.</li>
<li><strong>Undo</strong> and <strong>Redo</strong> (<code>⌘Z</code> and <code>⇧⌘Z</code> on a Mac, <code>Ctrl+Z</code> and <code>Ctrl+Shift+Z</code> on Windows and Linux). They&#39;re greyed out when there&#39;s nothing to undo or redo.</li>
<li>On the right: <strong>New project</strong> (<code>⌘N</code>), <strong>Open project</strong> (<code>⌘O</code>) and <strong>Save project</strong> (<code>⌘S</code>). Press <code>⇧⌘S</code> to save a copy under a new name.</li>
<li>The <strong>Notifications</strong> bell, with a number for new ones. It keeps everything that finished or failed. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/notifications/">Notifications</a>.</li>
<li>The <strong>Export</strong> button. Its label shows the current format, for example <strong>Export 16:9</strong>. Click it to choose a size: <strong>Small</strong>, <strong>Standard</strong> or <strong>High</strong>, each with an estimated file size. It&#39;s greyed out until there&#39;s a video on the timeline.</li>
</ul>
<p>While a video exports, a progress bar appears next to the buttons and <strong>Export</strong> becomes <strong>Cancel export</strong>. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/export/">Export</a>.</p>
<p>On a Mac, the window buttons (close, minimise, full screen) are at the top left. On Windows and Linux they&#39;re at the top right, and they follow the light or dark theme.</p>

<p>The Translate panel&#39;s title and button follow the language you translate to. If you pick Thai, they say <strong>Auto Thai subtitles</strong> and <strong>Make Thai subtitles</strong>.</p>
<h3 id="full-pages"><a class="anchor" href="#full-pages" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Full pages</h3><p><strong>Voices</strong>, <strong>Story</strong>, <strong>Downloads</strong> and <strong>Batch</strong> each replace the tools panel, preview, subtitles and timeline with their own page. Click any editor item in the rail (for example <strong>Media</strong>) to go back. Downloads and a batch keep going while you&#39;re on another page.</p>
<p>While <strong>Batch</strong> is processing, it uses the editor itself: a bar across the top says <strong>Batch running</strong>, and <strong>New</strong>, <strong>Open</strong> and <strong>Save</strong> wait until it&#39;s done. Your own project comes back afterwards. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/batch/#processing">Batch</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Settings</strong> fills the whole window. On the left there&#39;s <strong>Back to app</strong>, a <strong>Search settings</strong> box (<code>⌘F</code>), and the list of pages. Press <code>Esc</code> or click the <strong>×</strong> at the top right to go back to the editor.</p>
<h2 id="the-tools-panel"><a class="anchor" href="#the-tools-panel" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The tools panel</h2><p>The panel next to the rail shows the tools for the rail item you picked. It scrolls if the tools don&#39;t fit. Long jobs, like downloading or translating, show their progress right in the panel, so you can see what&#39;s happening without leaving your place.</p>
<h2 id="the-preview"><a class="anchor" href="#the-preview" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The preview</h2><p>The preview plays your video exactly as it will export: the format you chose, your subtitles in their style, the voice-over, and any objects.</p>
<ul>
<li>Click the video, or press <code>Space</code>, to play and pause.</li>
<li>Below the video: the current time, <strong>Back 5 seconds</strong> (<code>⇧←</code>), <strong>Play</strong> / <strong>Pause</strong>, <strong>Forward 5 seconds</strong> (<code>⇧→</code>), and the total length.</li>
<li>Objects (text, images, blur boxes) can be dragged on the preview to move them, and resized from their corner.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-subtitles-list"><a class="anchor" href="#the-subtitles-list" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The Subtitles list</h2><p>The panel on the right lists every subtitle line. The header shows how many lines there are and how many are translated, for example &quot;120 lines · 118 in Khmer&quot;.</p>
<p>Each line shows:</p>
<ul>
<li>Its <strong>time</strong> (start → end). Click the time to jump the video there.</li>
<li>The <strong>original line</strong> in grey, when there is one.</li>
<li>The <strong>translated line</strong> underneath. Click it and type to fix it. Lines not translated yet say &quot;Not translated yet&quot;.</li>
<li>Once you&#39;ve set up characters, a chip showing <strong>who says the line</strong>. Click it to pick another character, <strong>Default voice</strong>, or <strong>New character…</strong>.</li>
<li>A bin icon on hover to <strong>Delete line</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>The buttons at the top right: <strong>Import an .srt file</strong>, <strong>Find and replace</strong>, and <strong>Add a line at the playhead</strong> (a new 2-second line at the current time). Under them, <strong>Find in subtitles</strong> filters the list to lines that contain your words, in either language.</p>
<p>While the video plays, the list scrolls to keep the current line in view. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/subtitles/">Subtitles</a>.</p>
<h2 id="the-timeline"><a class="anchor" href="#the-timeline" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The timeline</h2><p>The timeline along the bottom has two tracks: <strong>Subtitles</strong> on top and <strong>Video</strong> below, with a time ruler above them.</p>
<ul>
<li>Click or drag on the ruler or a track to move the playhead (the line that marks the current time).</li>
<li><strong>Video clips</strong>: drag a clip to reorder it, or drag its edges to trim it.</li>
<li><strong>Subtitle blocks</strong>: drag to move a line, or drag its edges to change when it starts and ends.</li>
<li>The toolbar: <strong>Split at the playhead</strong> (<code>S</code>), <strong>Delete the selection</strong> (<code>Delete</code> or <code>Backspace</code>), then on the right <strong>Zoom out</strong>, a zoom slider, <strong>Zoom in</strong>, and <strong>Fit the whole video</strong>.</li>
<li>Hold <code>⌘</code> (or <code>Ctrl</code>) and scroll, or pinch on a trackpad, to zoom around the pointer.</li>
<li>Press <code>←</code> or <code>→</code> to step one frame, <code>Esc</code> to clear the selection.</li>
</ul>
<p>When something is selected, the toolbar tells you what you can do with it, for example &quot;Subtitle selected · drag to move, edges to retime&quot;.</p>
<h2 id="the-status-bar"><a class="anchor" href="#the-status-bar" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The status bar</h2><p>The thin bar along the very bottom of every page shows the whole job, start to finish:</p>
<p><strong>Video → Subtitles → Khmer → Characters → Voices → Style → Export</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Each step has a number, or a green tick when it&#39;s done.</li>
<li>Click a step to open its panel (from another page, it takes you back to the editor). <strong>Export</strong> opens the export sizes.</li>
<li><strong>Characters</strong>, <strong>Style</strong>, and <strong>Voices</strong> (while the voice-over is off) are marked <strong>optional</strong>. Subtitles alone make a finished video.</li>
<li>The <strong>Khmer</strong> step shows the language you translate to, so it reads <strong>Thai</strong> if you translate to Thai.</li>
<li>On the right, <strong>Next</strong> always points at the first step still to do, for example <strong>Next: Get the subtitles</strong> or <strong>Next: Export the video</strong>. When everything is done it says <strong>All done</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>On the right end:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Batch · 3/5</strong>, while a batch runs. Click it to open Batch.</li>
<li>The <strong>voice engine</strong>: <strong>Voice engine on</strong>, <strong>Voice engine off · Start</strong>, <strong>Voice engine not installed</strong>, <strong>installing…</strong> or <strong>starting…</strong>. Click it to start the engine, or to open its settings. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/voice-engine/">The voice engine</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Check for updates</strong>, or an update&#39;s progress, or <strong>Restart to update</strong> after you hide an update card. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/updates/">Updates</a>.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="light-dark-and-interface-size"><a class="anchor" href="#light-dark-and-interface-size" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Light, dark and interface size</h2><ul>
<li><strong>Theme</strong>: in <strong>Settings → Appearance</strong>, choose <strong>Dark</strong> (the default), <strong>Light</strong>, or <strong>System</strong> (follows your computer&#39;s setting).</li>
<li><strong>Interface size</strong>: makes everything bigger or smaller, like zooming a web page. Choose from 70% to 140% in <strong>Settings → Appearance</strong> (90% by default), or press <code>⌘+</code> / <code>⌘−</code>, and <code>⌘0</code> for 100%. On Windows and Linux use <code>Ctrl</code> instead of <code>⌘</code>. The same items are in the <strong>View</strong> menu.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="more-than-one-window"><a class="anchor" href="#more-than-one-window" aria-hidden="true">#</a>More than one window</h2><p>You can work on two projects side by side. Open a <strong>New Window</strong> with:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>File → New Window</strong>, or <code>⇧⌘N</code> (<code>Ctrl+Shift+N</code> on Windows and Linux).</li>
<li>Right-click Kroma in the Dock (Mac) or the taskbar (Windows) and choose <strong>New Window</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>Each window has its own project and its own autosave. The first window&#39;s project reopens next time you start Kroma. If you close an extra window whose project isn&#39;t saved to a file, Kroma asks <strong>Close this window?</strong> first: choose <strong>Cancel</strong> and press <code>⌘S</code> to keep your work.</p>
<p>Opening Kroma again while it&#39;s running doesn&#39;t start a second copy. It brings the open window to the front.</p>
<h2 id="autosave"><a class="anchor" href="#autosave" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Autosave</h2><p>Kroma saves your open project automatically a moment after every change, and reopens it when you start Kroma again (&quot;Reopened your last project.&quot;). To keep a project for later, or move it to another computer, save it as a <code>.kroma</code> file with <strong>Save project</strong>. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/projects-and-files/">Projects and files</a>.</p>
<p>All shortcuts are listed in <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/keyboard-shortcuts/">Keyboard shortcuts</a>.</p>
