<h2 id="the-status-bar"><a class="anchor" href="#the-status-bar" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The status bar</h2><p>The thin bar along the bottom of the window shows the whole job in seven steps:</p>
<p><strong>Video → Subtitles → Khmer → Characters → Voices → Style → Export</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>A step that&#39;s finished gets a green tick. The others show their number.</li>
<li><strong>Characters</strong>, <strong>Voices</strong> and <strong>Style</strong> say <em>optional</em>. A video with only subtitles is a finished video too.</li>
<li>Click a step to open its panel. <strong>Video</strong> opens <strong>Media</strong>, <strong>Subtitles</strong> and <strong>Khmer</strong> open <strong>Translate</strong>, <strong>Characters</strong> and <strong>Voices</strong> open <strong>Voice</strong>, <strong>Style</strong> opens <strong>Style</strong>, and <strong>Export</strong> opens the export sizes.</li>
<li>On the right, the <strong>Next: …</strong> button always points at the first step still to do, for example <strong>Next: Add a video</strong> or <strong>Next: Translate to Khmer</strong>. Press it to jump straight there.</li>
<li>When nothing is left to do, it says <strong>All done</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>If you translate into another language, the <strong>Khmer</strong> step shows that language&#39;s name instead (for example <strong>Thai</strong>).</p>
<h2 id="1-add-a-video"><a class="anchor" href="#1-add-a-video" aria-hidden="true">#</a>1. Add a video</h2><p>Press <strong>Next: Add a video</strong>, or click <strong>Media</strong> in the menu.</p>
<ol>
<li>Copy the video&#39;s link from YouTube, Bilibili, Douyin, TikTok, Facebook or another site. A whole share message works too, for example <code>【Video title】 https://b23.tv/…</code>.</li>
<li>Paste it into the box that says <strong>Paste a link or a share message</strong>. If the message has a title in it, Kroma shows it under the box (<strong>Title: …</strong>).</li>
<li>Press <strong>Get</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>A progress bar shows the download (<code>Downloading 45% · 3.2MiB/s · 00:12 left</code>). You can press the <strong>×</strong> to cancel. When it&#39;s done you&#39;ll see <strong>Downloaded.</strong>, or <strong>Downloaded, with the site&#39;s subtitles (…)</strong> when the site had its own subtitles.</p>
<p>Have the file already? Press <strong>Import</strong> in the <strong>Media</strong> section instead and pick it from your computer.</p>
<p>The first video you add goes straight onto the timeline and gives the project its name. If it&#39;s a tall (vertical) video, the project switches to the <strong>9:16</strong> format by itself. Read <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/downloading/">Downloading videos</a> for sites, quality, logins and where files are saved.</p>
<h2 id="2-get-the-subtitles-and-translate-them"><a class="anchor" href="#2-get-the-subtitles-and-translate-them" aria-hidden="true">#</a>2. Get the subtitles and translate them</h2><p>Press <strong>Next: Get the subtitles</strong>. This opens <strong>Translate</strong>.</p>
<ol>
<li>Check <strong>Spoken language</strong>. It starts on <strong>Chinese (Mandarin)</strong>. Pick <strong>Detect automatically</strong> if you&#39;re not sure.</li>
<li>Check <strong>Translate to</strong>. It starts on <strong>Khmer</strong>.</li>
<li>Optional but worth it: in <strong>Notes for the translator</strong>, write the names and who&#39;s who, like <code>李明 is the hero, write it លី មីង</code>.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Make Khmer subtitles</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>Kroma does two things in a row:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Gets the original lines.</strong> If the video came with the site&#39;s own Chinese subtitles, Kroma uses those and says <em>&quot;This video has the site&#39;s own Chinese subtitles, so speech-to-text is skipped.&quot;</em> Otherwise it listens to the video with speech-to-text in the cloud. You&#39;ll see a message like <strong>312 subtitle lines ready.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Translates them</strong>, then tells you <strong>Translated 312 lines to Khmer.</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Long movies take a minute or two. Both steps show a progress bar with a <strong>×</strong> to cancel. The <strong>Subtitles</strong> and <strong>Khmer</strong> steps turn green when they&#39;re done.</p>
<p>More about this: <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/subtitles/">Subtitles</a> and <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/translation/">Translation</a>.</p>
<h2 id="3-check-the-lines"><a class="anchor" href="#3-check-the-lines" aria-hidden="true">#</a>3. Check the lines</h2><p>Every line now shows in the <strong>Subtitles</strong> list on the right, with the original in grey and the translation below it.</p>
<ul>
<li>Click a line&#39;s time (like <code>00:12.40 → 00:15.00</code>) to jump the video there.</li>
<li>Click into the translation and type to fix it. Your change shows in the preview straight away.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Space</strong> to play and pause. The playing line is highlighted and the list follows it.</li>
<li>A name spelled wrong everywhere? Use <strong>Find and replace</strong> (the button at the top of the list) to fix it in every line at once.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="4-characters-and-voices-optional"><a class="anchor" href="#4-characters-and-voices-optional" aria-hidden="true">#</a>4. Characters and voices (optional)</h2><p>To give the video a voice-over, open <strong>Voice</strong>. Kroma can find the characters and give each one a different voice. When you start a voice-over, the status bar&#39;s <strong>Next</strong> button shows <strong>Finish the voice-over</strong> until every line has its voice. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/characters-and-voices/">Characters &amp; voices</a>.</p>
<p>Skip this step if you only want subtitles.</p>
<h2 id="5-style-the-subtitles-optional"><a class="anchor" href="#5-style-the-subtitles-optional" aria-hidden="true">#</a>5. Style the subtitles (optional)</h2><p>Open <strong>Style</strong> to choose a format (<strong>16:9</strong>, <strong>9:16</strong> or <strong>1:1</strong>), a look from the 33 presets (like <strong>Classic</strong>, <strong>Yellow</strong>, <strong>Moul gold</strong> or <strong>Dark box</strong>), an <strong>Animation</strong>, the size and the position. If the video already has Chinese subtitles burned into the picture, open <strong>Objects</strong> and press <strong>Hide original subtitles</strong>: Kroma finds them and blurs them. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/style-and-objects/">Style &amp; objects</a>.</p>
<h2 id="6-export"><a class="anchor" href="#6-export" aria-hidden="true">#</a>6. Export</h2><p>Press <strong>Next: Export the video</strong>, the <strong>Export</strong> step, or the <strong>Export 16:9</strong> button at the top right (it shows your format). Choose a size:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Small</strong>: for TikTok and Facebook.</li>
<li><strong>Standard</strong>: quick, good for YouTube.</li>
<li><strong>High</strong>: best quality, slower.</li>
</ul>
<p>Each one shows how big the file will be. The progress shows at the top right, with a <strong>Cancel export</strong> button. When it&#39;s finished, Kroma says <strong>Export finished</strong> and opens the folder with your video selected. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/export/">Export</a>.</p>
<h2 id="your-work-is-saved-for-you"><a class="anchor" href="#your-work-is-saved-for-you" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Your work is saved for you</h2><p>Kroma saves the open project a moment after every change, and reopens it the next time you start the app (<strong>Reopened your last project.</strong>). To keep a project as a file you can open later, press <strong>Save project</strong> (<code>⌘S</code>). See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/projects-and-files/">Projects &amp; files</a>.</p>
<h2 id="next-a-whole-series"><a class="anchor" href="#next-a-whole-series" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Next: a whole series</h2><p>Done one episode? For the rest of a series, paste the series&#39; link in <strong>Downloads</strong> (each episode becomes its own project), then let <strong>Batch</strong> take them through the same steps while you do something else. Start with <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/downloading/#the-download-center">Downloading videos</a> and <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/batch/">Batch</a>.</p>
