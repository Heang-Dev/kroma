33:T3103,<p>A voice-over reads your translated subtitles aloud, each line at its own time in the video. In a drama, every character can speak with a different voice: the mother gets a warm voice, the villain a dark one, the narrator a deep one.</p>
<p>Everything on this page happens in the <strong>Voice</strong> panel (left menu). The panel has three parts, top to bottom: <strong>Characters</strong>, the voice-over itself (called <strong>Default voice</strong> once you have characters), and <strong>Mix</strong>.</p>
<p>You need translated subtitles first. If there are none, the panel says <strong>Make the subtitles first.</strong></p>
<h2 id="characters"><a class="anchor" href="#characters" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Characters</h2><p>A character is a person who speaks in the video. Each character has a name, a colour and a voice. Lines that belong to a character are spoken in that character&#39;s voice. Lines with no character are spoken by the <strong>Default voice</strong>.</p>
<p>Characters are optional. A vlog or an explainer with one speaker works fine with only the default voice.</p>
<h3 id="find-characters-automatically"><a class="anchor" href="#find-characters-automatically" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Find characters automatically</h3><p>Press <strong>Find automatically</strong>. Kroma reads the dialogue (about 20 seconds) and:</p>
<ul>
<li>names each character the way the video does, like a name or a role (&quot;Mother&quot;, &quot;Boss&quot;, &quot;Narrator&quot;),</li>
<li>writes a short description, like &quot;old man, the grandfather&quot;,</li>
<li>decides who says each line,</li>
<li>picks a voice for each character, matching gender and age first, then mood.</li>
</ul>
<p>Some things to know:</p>
<ul>
<li>It reads the <strong>words only</strong>, not the sound. It works from the original lines (for example the Chinese), or from the translation when there&#39;s no original. So it&#39;s a good guess, not a sure answer. Check it and fix any line that&#39;s wrong.</li>
<li>It needs an AI key: your free Gemini key, or a Claude key (see <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/configuration/">Add your keys</a>). Without one you&#39;ll see: &quot;Finding characters needs your free Gemini key (Settings → Translation) or a Claude key.&quot;</li>
<li>It picks from the same kind of voice you already use. If your default voice is a designed voice (and the video is in Khmer), it picks designed voices. Otherwise it picks instant voices. It never picks a cloned voice.</li>
<li>If it can&#39;t tell who says a line, it gives the line to whoever spoke just before.</li>
<li>Long videos are read in parts of 250 lines. Names found in earlier parts are reused, so the same person keeps the same name.</li>
</ul>
<p>When it&#39;s done, a message says how many characters it found. The button now says <strong>Find again</strong>. If you press it again, characters with the same name keep the voice you already chose for them.</p>
<h3 id="add-a-character-by-hand"><a class="anchor" href="#add-a-character-by-hand" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Add a character by hand</h3><p>Press <strong>Add a character</strong>. A new character called &quot;Character 1&quot; (then &quot;Character 2&quot;, and so on) appears, using your default voice.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Rename</strong> it: click the name, type, and press Enter.</li>
<li>If you rename a character to a name that already exists, the two are merged into one.</li>
<li>A character with no lines shows a yellow note: &quot;No lines yet&quot;. Give it lines from the subtitles (see below).</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="remove-a-character"><a class="anchor" href="#remove-a-character" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Remove a character</h3><p>Press the bin icon next to the character. Their lines go back to the default voice.</p>
<h3 id="choose-a-voice-for-each-character"><a class="anchor" href="#choose-a-voice-for-each-character" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Choose a voice for each character</h3><p>Under each character there&#39;s a voice menu and a ▶ button. The menu opens with a search box: type &quot;king&quot;, &quot;girl&quot; or &quot;narrator&quot; to find a voice. The current voice has a ✓, and each voice has a line about how it sounds. The voices are in groups, in this order:</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Group</th>
<th>What&#39;s in it</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><strong>Your voices</strong></td>
<td>Voices you cloned. Shown only when you have some. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/voice-cloning/">Voice cloning</a>.</td>

<p>Press ▶ to hear a sample. Press it again (■) to stop. To hear every voice side by side, open the <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/voice-library/">Voice library</a>.</p>
<p>Each character row also shows how many lines that character has.</p>
<h3 id="change-who-says-a-line"><a class="anchor" href="#change-who-says-a-line" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Change who says a line</h3><p>Once you have characters, every line in the <strong>Subtitles</strong> list shows a small coloured name. Click it to open a menu:</p>
<ul>
<li>pick a character,</li>
<li>pick <strong>Default voice</strong>, or</li>
<li>pick <strong>New character…</strong> to make a new character and give it this line at once.</li>
</ul>
<p>If some lines have no character, the panel says, for example, &quot;12 lines have no character: the default voice speaks them.&quot;</p>
<h2 id="the-default-voice"><a class="anchor" href="#the-default-voice" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The default voice</h2><p>This part of the panel is called <strong>Khmer voice-over</strong> (or your language&#39;s name) until you add characters. Then it&#39;s called <strong>Default voice</strong>.</p>
<p>Choose the <strong>Voice type</strong>:</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Type</th>
<th>What it is</th>
<th>Good for</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><strong>Instant</strong></td>
<td>Microsoft&#39;s free voices, ready in seconds</td>
<td>Drafts, checking timing, and every language other than Khmer</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Designed</strong></td>
<td>The 36 Khmer preset voices from the voice engine</td>
<td>Natural, expressive Khmer</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td>Your own voices, made from a short recording</td>

<p>Designed and cloned voices are slower than instant ones, so make the final voice-over once your subtitles are final.</p>
<p>If your video&#39;s language isn&#39;t Khmer, the panel warns that designed and cloned voices are made for Khmer and may read your language with a Khmer accent. Use <strong>Instant</strong> for those languages.</p>
<h3 id="speed"><a class="anchor" href="#speed" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Speed</h3><p>With <strong>Instant</strong> voices you get a <strong>Speed</strong> slider, from -30% to +50% in steps of 5. The default is +10%. It changes how fast the instant voices read. Designed and cloned voices don&#39;t use this slider.</p>
<h2 id="make-the-voice-over"><a class="anchor" href="#make-the-voice-over" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Make the voice-over</h2><p>Press <strong>Make voice-over</strong>. Kroma speaks every translated line. When you have characters, it works through one voice at a time, so the progress shows, for example, &quot;Voice-over 2/3: Mother&quot;.</p>
<p>Under the button, <strong>Lines voiced</strong> shows how many lines have a voice, for example &quot;48 / 52&quot;.</p>
<p>When it&#39;s done, the <strong>Use the voice-over</strong> switch turns on and you hear the voice-over in the preview.</p>
<h3 id="only-changed-lines-are-redone"><a class="anchor" href="#only-changed-lines-are-redone" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Only changed lines are redone</h3><p>Kroma remembers each recording. So:</p>
<ul>
<li>editing a subtitle re-voices only that line,</li>
<li>changing a character&#39;s voice re-voices only that character&#39;s lines,</li>
<li>changing the speed re-voices only lines read by instant voices.</li>
</ul>
<p>When some lines are new, the button says <strong>Voice the 4 new lines</strong> (or however many). Press it to fill only those.</p>
<p>If a few lines fail (for example the internet drops), you&#39;ll see &quot;Voiced 40 lines; 3 not done. Press again to finish them.&quot; Press the button again.</p>
<h3 id="how-the-timing-fits"><a class="anchor" href="#how-the-timing-fits" aria-hidden="true">#</a>How the timing fits</h3><p>A translated line is often longer than the original. Kroma never cuts a line short. Instead:</p>
<ol>
<li>Each line starts when its subtitle starts.</li>
<li>If a line is too long for its gap, it&#39;s sped up, up to 1.6× its normal speed.</li>
<li>If it&#39;s still too long, it runs into the next line&#39;s time, and the next line starts a little later. The delay is caught up in the next pause.</li>
<li>If a line would end more than 1.2 seconds after the next subtitle starts, it&#39;s sped up harder, up to 2×, to stay with the picture.</li>
</ol>
<p>The preview and the exported video use exactly the same timing.</p>
<h2 id="mix"><a class="anchor" href="#mix" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Mix</h2><p>The <strong>Mix</strong> section decides what you hear under the voice-over.</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Setting</th>
<th>What it does</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><strong>Use the voice-over</strong></td>
<td>Turns the voice-over on or off, in the preview and in the export.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Remove the original voices</strong></td>
<td>Takes out the original speech and keeps the music and sound effects. On by default.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Quality</strong></td>
<td><strong>Fast</strong> (the default) removes speech well. <strong>Best</strong> is the cleanest, but slower than the video&#39;s length without a strong graphics card.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Music &amp; effects</strong></td>
<td>The volume of what&#39;s left after the voices are removed. Default 100%.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Original sound</strong></td>
<td>Shown when voice removal is off: the volume of the original sound under the voice-over. Default 15%.</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>Press <strong>Hear the first line</strong> to play the first voiced line.</p>
<h3 id="removing-the-original-voices"><a class="anchor" href="#removing-the-original-voices" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Removing the original voices</h3><p>When you make a voice-over, Kroma removes the original voices <strong>automatically</strong> right after, so you don&#39;t hear the original speech under the new voice. Music and sound effects stay.</p>
<ul>
<li>It runs on your computer, not online. It takes about 40 seconds per 5 minutes of video on a recent Mac or PC (with <strong>Fast</strong>).</li>
<li>The first time, it downloads the tool and the voice-removal model. This takes longer, once.</li>
<li>It needs a free tool called <strong>uv</strong>. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/installation/">Installation</a>. Without it you&#39;ll see &quot;Removing voices needs uv&quot; and how to install it (on a Mac, <code>brew install uv</code>).</li>
<li>It runs once per video. After that the result is saved, so it&#39;s instant.</li>
<li>Changing <strong>Quality</strong> runs it again with the new quality.</li>
<li>If you made a voice-over before this was automatic, it runs when you <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/export/">export</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>If it fails, you&#39;ll see &quot;The original voices are still in&quot;. The original sound then plays quietly under the voice-over. Try again by turning <strong>Remove the original voices</strong> off and on in <strong>Mix</strong>.</p>
<p>To keep the original sound (for example, a narrator over a music video), turn <strong>Remove the original voices</strong> off and set the <strong>Original sound</strong> volume.</p>
