2b:T4826,<p>Every project starts with a video. There are two places to get one:</p>
<ul>
<li>The <strong>Media</strong> panel (the first item in the menu on the left), for <strong>one video</strong>: download it from a link, or import a file you already have.</li>
<li>The <strong>Download Center</strong> (<strong>Downloads</strong> in the left menu), for <strong>many videos</strong>: a whole series, a collection, a playlist or a channel, or a folder of episodes you already have. Each video becomes its own project. See <a href="#the-download-center">The Download Center</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>The rest of this page starts with the Media panel.</p>
<h2 id="download-from-a-link"><a class="anchor" href="#download-from-a-link" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Download from a link</h2><ol>
<li>Open <strong>Media</strong>.</li>
<li>In <strong>Download from a link</strong>, paste the link into the box that says <strong>Paste a link or a share message</strong>.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Get</strong> (or press Enter).</li>
</ol>
<p>Under the box, <strong>Download many (series, playlist, channel) →</strong> opens the Download Center.</p>
<p>Kroma downloads with <strong>yt-dlp</strong>, a free tool that knows over 1,000 video sites. The panel names the common ones: YouTube, Bilibili, Douyin, Youku, iQiyi, Facebook and TikTok.</p>
<p>While it works you&#39;ll see what&#39;s happening:</p>

<p>Press the <strong>×</strong> next to the progress to cancel. When it&#39;s done, Kroma says <strong>Downloaded.</strong>, or <strong>Downloaded, with the site&#39;s subtitles (zh-Hans)</strong> (or another language code) when the site had subtitles.</p>
<p>If the box doesn&#39;t hold a link, Kroma says: <em>&quot;Paste a link that starts with https:// (a share message with a link in it works too).&quot;</em></p>
<h2 id="share-messages"><a class="anchor" href="#share-messages" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Share messages</h2><p>Apps like Douyin and Bilibili don&#39;t give you a plain link. Their <strong>Share</strong> button copies a whole message, such as:</p>
<p><code>【Is a hamburger really junk food?】 https://b23.tv/abc123</code></p>
<p>Paste the whole thing. Kroma finds the link inside it, and also reads the title. You&#39;ll see <strong>Title: …</strong> under the box before you press <strong>Get</strong>. That title becomes the video&#39;s name and the project&#39;s name.</p>
<p>Kroma understands titles inside <code>【…】</code>, <code>《…》</code>, <code>「…」</code> and <code>『…』</code>, or plain words before the link. It drops the app&#39;s extra words (like &quot;copy and open Douyin&quot;) on its own.</p>
<p>Kroma also cleans the link before downloading:</p>
<ul>
<li>It removes the parts that only track who shared it.</li>
<li>Bilibili links with a language in them (like <code>bilibili.com/en/video/…</code>) are fixed so they work.</li>
<li>For a Bilibili video in several parts, the part number (<code>p=</code>) is kept, so you get the part you shared.</li>
<li>A YouTube link from a playlist downloads only that one video, not the whole list.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-site-s-own-subtitles"><a class="anchor" href="#the-site-s-own-subtitles" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The site&#39;s own subtitles</h2><p>Many sites have subtitles of their own. Kroma asks for them with every download, Chinese first (Simplified, then Traditional, including Bilibili&#39;s AI subtitles), and English too.</p>
<p>When a video comes with subtitles, its row in <strong>Media</strong> shows a small badge with the language codes, like <code>zh-Hans</code>.</p>
<p>Why it matters: when the site has subtitles, <strong>Make Khmer subtitles</strong> uses them and skips speech-to-text: Chinese first, then English, then any other language the site has. That&#39;s faster, and the lines are usually exact. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/subtitles/">Subtitles</a>.</p>
<p>If the site refuses the subtitles (this happens when it&#39;s busy), Kroma says <strong>Retrying without the site&#39;s subtitles…</strong> and gets the video anyway.</p>
<h2 id="quality"><a class="anchor" href="#quality" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Quality</h2><p>Kroma picks the best version up to <strong>1080p</strong> and saves it as an <strong>MP4</strong> file when the site has one. 1080p is the size of the exports, so a bigger download wouldn&#39;t help. In the Download Center you can choose <strong>720p</strong> or <strong>480p</strong> instead.</p>
<p>Some sites, like Bilibili, only give 1080p when you&#39;re signed in. Others only play some videos for members. To use your logins:</p>
<ol>
<li>Sign in to the site in your web browser.</li>
<li>In Kroma, open <strong>Settings → Downloads</strong>.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Use my logins from</strong>, choose your browser: <strong>Chrome</strong>, <strong>Safari</strong>, <strong>Firefox</strong>, <strong>Edge</strong> or <strong>Brave</strong>. The default is <strong>None · public videos only</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>Your computer may ask you to allow this. Safari also needs Full Disk Access for Kroma.</p>
<h2 id="where-downloads-go"><a class="anchor" href="#where-downloads-go" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Where downloads go</h2><p>Each download from the <strong>Media</strong> panel gets its own folder (named with a number) inside the download folder, together with the site&#39;s subtitles. The Download Center uses a folder per series instead (see <a href="#one-project-per-episode">One project per episode</a>). The default download folder is:</p>
<ul>
<li>Mac: <code>Movies/Kroma Studio/downloads</code></li>
<li>Windows and Linux: <code>Videos/Kroma Studio/downloads</code></li>
</ul>
<p>To see them, open <strong>Settings → Folders</strong> and press <strong>Open</strong> next to <strong>Download folder</strong>. Press <strong>Change…</strong> to use another folder, or <strong>Reset</strong> to go back to the default. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/settings/">Settings</a>.</p>
<h2 id="import-a-file"><a class="anchor" href="#import-a-file" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Import a file</h2><p>Already have the video? In <strong>Media</strong>, press <strong>Import</strong> at the top right of the <strong>Media</strong> section. You can pick several files at once.</p>
<p>Kroma opens these kinds of video files: <code>.mp4</code>, <code>.mov</code>, <code>.m4v</code>, <code>.mkv</code>, <code>.webm</code>, <code>.avi</code>, <code>.flv</code> and <code>.ts</code>.</p>
<p>Imported files stay where they are. Kroma doesn&#39;t copy them, so don&#39;t move or delete them while you&#39;re working on the project.</p>
<p>A file with only sound (no picture) can&#39;t go on the video track yet. Kroma tells you: <em>&quot;Audio-only files can&#39;t go on the video track yet.&quot;</em></p>
<h2 id="more-than-one-clip"><a class="anchor" href="#more-than-one-clip" aria-hidden="true">#</a>More than one clip</h2><p>The <strong>Media</strong> section lists every video in the project. Each row shows the name, the length, the picture size (like <code>1920×1080</code>) and the subtitle badge if it has one.</p>
<ul>
<li>The <strong>first</strong> video you add goes onto the timeline by itself. It also names the project, and if it&#39;s a tall video the format switches to <strong>9:16</strong>.</li>
<li>Videos you add after that only go into the list. Press the <strong>+</strong> on a row (<strong>Add to the end of the timeline</strong>) to put it after the others.</li>
<li>On the timeline, drag a clip to change the order, and drag its edges to trim it. See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/subtitles/#the-timeline">Subtitles</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>One thing to know: <strong>Make Khmer subtitles</strong> works on the video of the <strong>first clip</strong> on the timeline. For a second video, see <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/subtitles/">Subtitles</a>.</p>
<h2 id="the-download-center"><a class="anchor" href="#the-download-center" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The Download Center</h2><p>The <strong>Download Center</strong> downloads many videos at once, and makes a Kroma project for each one, ready to translate and voice. Open it with <strong>Downloads</strong> in the left menu. On the left you add videos; on the right is the <strong>Queue</strong> (or <strong>Supported sites</strong>).</p>
<h3 id="add-videos"><a class="anchor" href="#add-videos" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Add videos</h3><ol>
<li>Under <strong>Add videos</strong>, paste one or more links, one per line: a single video, a whole series, a collection (合集), a playlist, a multi-part upload, or a channel. Share messages work too, like <code>【第1集】 https://b23.tv/…</code>.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Find videos</strong> (or ⌘Enter / Ctrl+Enter).</li>
</ol>
<p>Kroma lists every video behind each link, grouped by series or playlist, with how many it has, for example <em>Playlist · 40 videos · 40 picked</em>. Titles fill in as Kroma reads them. A long playlist or channel lists its first videos; press <strong>Load more</strong> for the rest.</p>
<p>Everything is ticked to start with. Untick what you don&#39;t want, or use the group&#39;s buttons to pick all or none. Videos already in the queue say <strong>In queue</strong>. Nothing downloads until you press <strong>Download</strong>.</p>
<p>A YouTube channel link (<code>youtube.com/@name</code>) lists the channel&#39;s videos.</p>
<h3 id="options"><a class="anchor" href="#options" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Options</h3><p><strong>Options</strong> apply to the videos you download next:</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Option</th>
<th>Default</th>
<th>What it does</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><strong>Quality</strong></td>
<td><strong>1080p</strong></td>
<td><strong>1080p</strong>, <strong>720p</strong> or <strong>480p</strong>. Some sites only give 1080p when you&#39;re signed in (see <a href="#quality">Quality</a>).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Site subtitles</strong></td>
<td>On</td>
<td>Downloads the site&#39;s own subtitles too, when it has them.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Make a project for each video</strong></td>
<td>On</td>
<td>Makes a <code>.kroma</code> project for every video, with the video on the timeline.</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>Then press <strong>Download … videos</strong>.</p>
<h3 id="the-queue"><a class="anchor" href="#the-queue" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The queue</h3><p>The <strong>Queue</strong> lists every video, grouped by series, with a summary like <em>12 done · 2 downloading · 30 waiting</em>. Each video shows <strong>Waiting</strong>, its progress with speed and time left, <strong>Downloaded · project ready</strong>, <strong>Already downloaded</strong>, or why it failed.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Downloads at the same time</strong>: <strong>1 at a time</strong>, <strong>2 at a time</strong> (the default) or <strong>3 at a time</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Pause</strong> stops new downloads from starting (those running finish); <strong>Resume</strong> carries on.</li>
<li><strong>Retry failed</strong> tries every failed video again. <strong>Clear finished</strong> takes finished videos off the list.</li>
<li>On each video: <strong>Open project</strong>, <strong>Retry</strong> (when it failed), <strong>Cancel</strong>, <strong>Show in folder</strong>, and <strong>Remove from the list (the files stay)</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>The queue keeps going while you work on other pages, and picks up where it stopped after a restart. You get a notification for each video and a summary when the queue is done.</p>
<p><strong>Nothing downloads twice.</strong> Kroma remembers every video it downloaded. Add the same series again and the videos you have say <strong>Already downloaded</strong>, so only new episodes download. Removing a video from the list lets you download it again.</p>
<p><strong>When a site asks for a break.</strong> Downloading many videos in a row can make a site slow you down (Bilibili, or YouTube asking to confirm you&#39;re not a bot). Kroma then pauses the queue and says so: wait a while (Bilibili: a few minutes; YouTube: up to an hour) and press <strong>Resume</strong>, or turn on browser logins in <strong>Settings → Downloads</strong>.</p>
<h3 id="one-project-per-episode"><a class="anchor" href="#one-project-per-episode" aria-hidden="true">#</a>One project per episode</h3><p>With <strong>Make a project for each video</strong> on, every video becomes its own project, numbered in a folder named after the series:</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>What</th>
<th>Where</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>The videos</td>
<td>The download folder, in a folder for the series: <code>&lt;series&gt;/07 - &lt;title&gt; [&lt;id&gt;].mp4</code>, with the site&#39;s subtitles next to them</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>The projects</td>
<td><code>projects/&lt;series&gt;/07 - &lt;title&gt;.kroma</code> in the Kroma Studio library folder</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>The number keeps the episodes in order, with as many digits as the series needs (<code>07</code> in a series of 12, <code>007</code> in one of 250). Single videos have no number. The projects open ready to translate: the video is on the timeline, and a tall video starts in <strong>9:16</strong>.</p>
<p>Because every episode of a series shares a folder, they also share one <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/translation/#series-glossary">series glossary</a>, and covers read the episode number from the name (<a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/posting/#episode-covers">Episode covers</a>).</p>
<h3 id="your-own-files"><a class="anchor" href="#your-own-files" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Your own files</h3><p>Already have the episodes? Under <strong>Your own files</strong>, press <strong>Import files…</strong> or <strong>Import a folder…</strong>. Each video becomes its own project, in natural order (EP1, EP2 … EP10, or 第2集 before 第10集).</p>
<ul>
<li>A folder is read directly (not the folders inside it), for <code>mp4</code>, <code>mov</code>, <code>mkv</code>, <code>webm</code>, <code>m4v</code>, <code>avi</code> and <code>flv</code> files.</li>
<li>Subtitle files with the same name come with the video: <code>EP1.srt</code>, or with a language, <code>EP1.zh.srt</code>. Files with subtitles show <strong>SRT</strong>.</li>
<li>Tick the files you want and press <strong>Make … projects</strong>. The projects go in <code>projects/&lt;folder name&gt;/</code>.</li>
<li>Kroma uses the files where they are. Don&#39;t move or delete them.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="send-them-to-batch"><a class="anchor" href="#send-them-to-batch" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Send them to Batch</h3><p>Tick finished videos in the queue and press <strong>Process … selected</strong> to send them to <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/batch/">Batch</a>, which runs every step on them (subtitles, translation, voice-over, export…).</p>
<h3 id="supported-sites"><a class="anchor" href="#supported-sites" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Supported sites</h3><p><strong>Supported sites</strong> (next to <strong>Queue</strong>) shows the sites people ask about most, Chinese sites first, with what works on each and <strong>Open site</strong> to visit it:</p>

</tr>

<p>Under them, <strong>Every site</strong> lists every site the downloader can read, with <strong>Search sites</strong>. Protected (DRM) VIP episodes can&#39;t be downloaded by any app like this one.</p>
<h2 id="when-a-download-fails"><a class="anchor" href="#when-a-download-fails" aria-hidden="true">#</a>When a download fails</h2><p>Kroma explains what went wrong in plain words:</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Message</th>
<th>What to do</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><em>This video is protected (encrypted), so it can&#39;t be downloaded.</em></td>
<td>The site uses DRM (copy protection), common on paid streaming. Kroma can&#39;t download these.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><em>This link isn&#39;t a video page Kroma can download from…</em></td>
<td>Open the video itself and copy its link, not the channel or home page.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><em>This video needs you to be signed in…</em></td>
<td>Choose your browser in <strong>Settings → Downloads</strong> (see <a href="#quality">Quality</a>).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><em>The video isn&#39;t available any more…</em></td>
<td>It was removed, or the link is wrong.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><em>This video isn&#39;t available from your country.</em></td>
<td>The site blocks your country.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><em>The connection failed…</em></td>
<td>Check your internet and try again.</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>In the Download Center, the reason shows on the video in the queue, and in the <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/notifications/">bell</a>. If the whole queue pauses, see <em>When a site asks for a break</em> in <a href="#the-queue">The queue</a>.</p>
<p>If the top of the window says <strong>Missing yt-dlp</strong>, the download tool isn&#39;t installed. It shows the command to install it (on a Mac, <code>brew install yt-dlp</code>). See <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/troubleshooting/">Troubleshooting</a>.</p>
