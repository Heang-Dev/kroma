32:T2be1,<p><strong>Batch</strong> does the whole job for many videos at once. You choose the steps one time (subtitles, translation, voice-over, style, export, covers, post text), press <strong>Process</strong>, and every video goes through them, one after another, while you do something else. It stops before posting, so you can check each video first.</p>
<p>Open it with <strong>Batch</strong> in the left menu. The page has two tabs: <strong>Process</strong>, where videos are worked on, and <strong>Ready to post</strong>, where finished videos wait.</p>
<h2 id="a-whole-series-start-to-finish"><a class="anchor" href="#a-whole-series-start-to-finish" aria-hidden="true">#</a>A whole series, start to finish</h2><p>Batch is made for series. A typical run:</p>
<ol>
<li>In the <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/downloading/#the-download-center">Download Center</a>, paste the series&#39; link, tick the episodes and press <strong>Download</strong>. Each episode becomes its own project, in a folder named after the series.</li>
<li>Translate the first episode by hand in the editor and fix the names. Kroma fills the <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/translation/#series-glossary">series glossary</a>, so every later episode spells the names the same way.</li>
<li>In <strong>Batch</strong>, add the other episodes, choose the steps, and press <strong>Process</strong>.</li>
<li>Check each video in <strong>Ready to post</strong>, choose where it goes, and post.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="adding-videos"><a class="anchor" href="#adding-videos" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Adding videos</h2><p>Batch works on Kroma projects (<code>.kroma</code> files). Under <strong>Videos to process</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Add from downloads</strong> lists the finished videos from the Download Center that have a project. Tick the ones you want (<strong>All</strong> / <strong>None</strong> help), then press <strong>Add … to the list</strong>. Videos already in the list say <strong>In the list</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Add projects…</strong> picks any project files. The dialog opens in the <code>projects</code> folder, where the Download Center makes them.</li>
</ul>
<p>You can also send videos from the Download Center: tick finished downloads in its queue and press <strong>Process … selected</strong>.</p>
<p>A project is never added twice. Videos run one at a time, in the order they were added.</p>
<h2 id="choosing-the-steps"><a class="anchor" href="#choosing-the-steps" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Choosing the steps</h2><p><strong>Steps</strong> are the same for every video. Turn on the ones you want:</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table>
<thead>
<tr>

<th>What it does</th>
</tr>

</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Translate</strong></td>
<td>Translates every line into <strong>Translate to</strong>, with the translator chosen in <strong>Settings → Translation</strong>.</td>

<tr>

<td>Works out who says each line, so each character can have their own voice.</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td>Reads the translated lines aloud. <strong>Voices</strong>: <strong>One voice</strong> for every line, or <strong>Each character</strong> (needs <strong>Find characters</strong>). <p>Under the list, Kroma sums up what will happen, for example <em>&quot;5 steps: getting subtitles → translating → exporting 16:9 → exporting 9:16 → writing the posts. Then it waits in Ready to post.&quot;</em></p>
<p>The steps always run in this order. Removing the original voices comes before the voice-over, so the voice-over doesn&#39;t do it again. A video with no burned-in subtitles isn&#39;t a failure: <strong>Hide original subtitles</strong> notes <em>No burned-in subtitles found</em> and the batch carries on.</p>
<p>Your choices are kept for next time.</p>
<h2 id="processing"><a class="anchor" href="#processing" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Processing</h2><p>Press <strong>Process … videos</strong>. While it runs:</p>
<ul>
<li>A bar across the top of the window says <strong>Batch running</strong>, the video and the step it&#39;s on, with <strong>Open Batch</strong> and <strong>Stop</strong>. The status bar at the bottom shows it too.</li>
<li>Batch uses the editor to do the work, so the editor can&#39;t be used until it&#39;s done. Your own project is put aside, with its undo history, and comes back afterwards.</li>
<li>After every step, the project is saved. If something fails, nothing done so far is lost.</li>
<li>You get a notification for each finished video, and a summary at the end, like <strong>Batch finished: 5 processed · 1 failed</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Stop</strong> cancels the step that&#39;s running and stops the batch. Press <strong>Resume</strong> later to carry on where it stopped. If you quit Kroma during a batch, the video that was running shows as stopped when you open Kroma again, ready to resume.</p>
<h2 id="the-list"><a class="anchor" href="#the-list" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The list</h2><p>Videos are grouped by run (each press of <strong>Process</strong>), newest first, with <strong>Not started</strong> at the top. Each row shows the video, a chip for each step (done, running, failed, or skipped), and its status: <strong>Waiting</strong>, the step it&#39;s on, <strong>Failed at …</strong>, <strong>Stopped at …</strong>, <strong>Ready to post</strong> or <strong>Posted</strong>.</p>
<p>Above the list:</p>
<ul>
<li>Filters: <strong>All</strong>, <strong>Running</strong>, <strong>Waiting</strong>, <strong>Failed</strong>, <strong>Ready to post</strong> and <strong>Posted</strong>, and <strong>Search videos</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Retry failed</strong> runs every failed video again from the step that failed.</li>
<li><strong>Clear finished</strong> removes finished and failed videos from the list. The projects and exports stay.</li>
</ul>
<p>On each row:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Retry from …</strong> (failed videos) carries on from the failed step. The steps before it stay done.</li>
<li><strong>Open project at …</strong> opens the project in the editor, on the step&#39;s panel, to fix something by hand.</li>
<li><strong>⋯</strong> has <strong>Show … export in Finder</strong>, <strong>Step details</strong> and <strong>Remove from the list</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>The list is kept, so after a restart you see where everything stopped. It keeps the last 20 runs.</p>
<h3 id="step-details"><a class="anchor" href="#step-details" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Step details</h3><p>Click a row (or <strong>See why</strong> next to a failure) to open its details on the right: every step from top to bottom with when it ran, how long it took and what it said (how many lines were translated, the exported file, or the error).</p>
<ul>
<li>A failed step has <strong>Retry from here</strong> and <strong>Fix by hand</strong>.</li>
<li>A finished step has <strong>Run again from …</strong>, which runs that step and every step after it again. Useful after you change a setting, such as the style.</li>
<li><strong>Exports</strong> lists the exported files, each with <strong>Show in Finder</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="ready-to-post"><a class="anchor" href="#ready-to-post" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Ready to post</h2><p>Finished videos wait in <strong>Ready to post</strong>. Each card shows the video, its exports, and a row for <strong>YouTube</strong>, <strong>Facebook</strong> and <strong>TikTok</strong>:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Tick where it goes.</strong> Connected accounts are ticked to start with.</li>
<li><strong>Choose the format</strong> for each platform: <strong>16:9</strong> or <strong>9:16</strong>. YouTube starts on 16:9, Facebook and TikTok on 9:16. If a format isn&#39;t exported yet, Kroma exports it when you post.</li>
<li>Open <strong>Post text</strong> to read and edit the title, text and hashtags for each platform.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Post</strong> on the card, or tick several cards and press <strong>Post selected</strong> at the top.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>YouTube visibility</strong> (<strong>Public</strong>, <strong>Unlisted</strong> or <strong>Private</strong>) and <strong>TikTok</strong> (<strong>Post now</strong> or <strong>To drafts</strong>) at the top apply to everything you post from here. Each platform gets the file in its own format. Facebook goes up as a Reel when its video is 9:16 and 90 seconds or shorter.</p>
<p>After posting, the card shows each platform&#39;s result, and the video moves to <strong>Posted</strong>. <strong>Post again</strong> posts it once more; <strong>Clear posted</strong> removes posted videos from the list.</p>
<p>Posting needs Upload-Post set up once: see <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/posting/#set-up-posting-once">Posting</a>. Only post videos you have the right to use.</p>
