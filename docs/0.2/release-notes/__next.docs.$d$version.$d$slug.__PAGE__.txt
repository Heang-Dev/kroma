33:T2b4f,<p>What changed in each release of the 0.2 series, newest first.</p>
<h2 id="0-2-in-short"><a class="anchor" href="#0-2-in-short" aria-hidden="true">#</a>0.2 in short</h2><p>0.2 is about <strong>whole series</strong>. Since 0.1, Kroma has grown from one video at a time into a tool for a drama with forty episodes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/downloading/#the-download-center">Download Center</a></strong>: paste a series, a collection, a playlist or a channel, tick the episodes, and each one becomes its own numbered project. Import a folder of episodes you already have the same way.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/batch/">Batch</a></strong>: choose the steps once (subtitles, translation, voice-over, hiding the original subtitles, style, export, covers, post text) and every episode goes through them, one after another, then waits in <strong>Ready to post</strong> with the right format for each platform.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/style-and-objects/#hide-original-subtitles">Hide original subtitles</a></strong>: Kroma finds the Chinese subtitles burned into the video and blurs exactly those.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/style-and-objects/#animation">Animated captions</a></strong>, <strong>33 subtitle styles</strong>, seven Khmer display fonts, shadows and glows, and <strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/style-and-objects/#my-styles">My styles</a></strong> for your own looks.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/translation/#series-glossary">Series glossary</a></strong>: one spelling for every name, in every episode.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/posting/#episode-covers">Episode covers</a></strong> with the episode number, in 16:9 and 9:16.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/story/">Story studio</a></strong>, Kroma&#39;s own <strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/voice-engine/">voice engine</a></strong> built in, <strong><a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/notifications/">notifications</a></strong> with a bell and sounds, searchable dropdowns, and more reliable updates.</li>
</ul>
<p>Projects from 0.1 open in 0.2 and look exactly the same. Moving up needs nothing from you: see the <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/upgrade/">Upgrade guide</a>. The 0.1.x releases that led here are in the 0.1 docs (use the version menu at the top of the sidebar) and on the <a href="/kroma/changelog/">Changelog</a>.</p>

<p>Numbers are compared one part at a time, so 0.2.10 is newer than 0.2.9, and 0.2.0 is newer than 0.1.13.</p>
<h2 id="which-version-you-have"><a class="anchor" href="#which-version-you-have" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Which version you have</h2><p>Open <strong>Settings → About</strong> (⌘, or Ctrl+, then <strong>About</strong>). The top line shows it, for example <strong>Kroma Studio 0.2.0</strong>. Under <strong>Updates</strong>, the line next to <strong>Check for updates</strong> also says whether you have the latest version. The version is also shown at the bottom of the Settings sidebar.</p>
<h2 id="getting-a-new-version"><a class="anchor" href="#getting-a-new-version" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Getting a new version</h2><p>Kroma updates itself: see <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/updates/">Updates</a>. To install a specific version by hand, see the <a href="/kroma/docs/0.2/upgrade/">Upgrade guide</a>. Every release, with its downloads, is also on <a href="https://github.com/Heang-Dev/kroma/releases" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">GitHub Releases</a>.</p>
<h2 id="releases"><a class="anchor" href="#releases" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Releases</h2><h2 id="0-2-3"><a class="anchor" href="#0-2-3" aria-hidden="true">#</a>0.2.3</h2><p><em>November 28, 2026</em> · <a href="https://github.com/Heang-Dev/kroma/releases/tag/v0.2.3" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">GitHub</a></p>
<p>Notification banners on the Mac.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Notification banners on the Mac.</strong> Kroma now asks your Mac for permission to show notifications, and appears in <strong>System Settings → Notifications</strong> as <strong>Kroma Studio</strong>. Before, macOS refused Kroma&#39;s notifications without asking. After updating, press <strong>Send test</strong> in <strong>Settings → Notifications</strong> and choose <strong>Allow</strong> when your Mac asks; then pick <strong>Banners</strong> for Kroma Studio in System Settings.</li>
<li><strong>Kroma tells you when notifications are blocked.</strong> If your computer refuses Kroma&#39;s notifications, a notice says so with <strong>Open settings</strong>, and <strong>Settings → Notifications</strong> shows a warning until one gets through.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="0-2-2"><a class="anchor" href="#0-2-2" aria-hidden="true">#</a>0.2.2</h2><p><em>November 28, 2026</em> · <a href="https://github.com/Heang-Dev/kroma/releases/tag/v0.2.2" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">GitHub</a></p>
<p>Cancelling a voice-over really stops it.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fixed: a cancelled voice-over kept the voice engine busy.</strong> Before, pressing cancel stopped it in Kroma, but the voice engine went on voicing every remaining line, so your next voice-over sat at <strong>Waiting in line</strong> until it finished. Now the engine stops after the line it&#39;s speaking, and the next voice-over starts straight away.</li>
</ul>
<p>Notification banners on the Mac, and cancelling a voice-over really stops it.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Notification banners on the Mac.</strong> Kroma now asks your Mac for permission to show notifications, and appears in <strong>System Settings → Notifications</strong> as <strong>Kroma Studio</strong>. Before, macOS refused Kroma&#39;s notifications without asking. After updating, press <strong>Send test</strong> in <strong>Settings → Notifications</strong> and choose <strong>Allow</strong> when your Mac asks; then pick <strong>Banners</strong> for Kroma Studio in System Settings.</li>
<li><strong>Kroma tells you when notifications are blocked.</strong> If your computer refuses Kroma&#39;s notifications, a notice says so with <strong>Open settings</strong>, and <strong>Settings → Notifications</strong> shows a warning until one gets through.</li>
<li><strong>Cancelling a voice-over stops it in the voice engine.</strong> Before, the engine kept voicing the cancelled lines, and the next voice-over waited at <strong>Waiting in line</strong> until it finished. Now the cancelled one stops after the line being spoken, and the next starts right away.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="0-2-1"><a class="anchor" href="#0-2-1" aria-hidden="true">#</a>0.2.1</h2><p><em>November 28, 2026</em> · <a href="https://github.com/Heang-Dev/kroma/releases/tag/v0.2.1" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">GitHub</a></p>
<p>Notifications you hear and see, even when your Mac hides them.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>The notification sound always plays.</strong> On the Mac, Kroma now plays the sound you chose in <strong>Settings → Notifications</strong> itself, inside Kroma and while you&#39;re in another app, even when macOS mutes Kroma&#39;s notifications. You never hear it twice.</li>
<li><strong>Unread count on the Dock icon.</strong> Kroma&#39;s icon shows how many notifications you haven&#39;t seen, so finished or failed work stands out even without banners. It bounces once when something fails while you&#39;re in another app.</li>
<li><strong>Help when banners don&#39;t pop up.</strong> <strong>Settings → Notifications → Not seeing them?</strong> explains the alert style (<strong>Banners</strong> or <strong>Alerts</strong>) and Focus, and <strong>Notification log</strong> shows what Kroma sent and whether your computer showed it.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="0-2-0"><a class="anchor" href="#0-2-0" aria-hidden="true">#</a>0.2.0</h2><p><em>November 28, 2026</em> · <a href="https://github.com/Heang-Dev/kroma/releases/tag/v0.2.0" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">GitHub</a></p>
<p>Tools for whole series: hide the original subtitles automatically, animated captions, one spelling for every name, your own styles, and numbered episode covers.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hide original subtitles.</strong> In <strong>Objects</strong>, <strong>Hide original subtitles</strong> now finds the Chinese subtitles burned into the video and puts a blur box exactly over them for the whole video, whether the video uses <strong>Blur</strong>, <strong>Bars</strong> or <strong>Crop</strong>. If it finds none it says so. Batch can do it too: turn on <strong>Hide original subtitles</strong> in its steps.</li>
<li><strong>Animated captions.</strong> In <strong>Style → Subtitle style → Animation</strong>, choose how each line appears: <strong>Fade in</strong>, <strong>Pop</strong> or <strong>Slide up</strong>. Or let the words follow the voice: <strong>Spoken word</strong> lights up the word being said (pick its colour in <strong>Spoken word colour</strong>), and <strong>Word by word</strong> shows words as they&#39;re said. The preview and the exported video move the same way.</li>
<li><strong>Series glossary.</strong> In <strong>Translate → Series glossary</strong>, names and terms get one spelling that every episode in the same folder uses. Kroma fills it in after a series&#39; first translation, <strong>Find names</strong> adds more, and you can fix or remove any name. Gemini and Claude follow it; with Google Translate, the names are swapped in before translating.</li>
<li><strong>Save as my style.</strong> In <strong>Style</strong>, <strong>Save as my style</strong> keeps your whole subtitle look (font, colours, size, position, animation) under <strong>My styles</strong>, for every project and for Batch.</li>
<li><strong>Episode covers.</strong> In <strong>Post → Cover</strong>, make a thumbnail with the episode number (<strong>ភាគ ៥</strong>, <strong>EP 5</strong>, <strong>Part 5</strong> or your own word) and the series title, in four designs (<strong>Badge</strong>, <strong>Big number</strong>, <strong>Banner</strong>, <strong>Minimal</strong>) and any colour. The number is read from the episode&#39;s name (&quot;05 - …&quot;, &quot;EP12&quot;, &quot;第27集&quot;). Kroma picks a sharp frame, keeps hidden subtitles blurred, and <strong>Save covers</strong> saves 16:9 (YouTube) and 9:16 (TikTok, Reels) covers next to the exported videos. Batch can make covers for a whole series with <strong>Make covers</strong>.</li>
