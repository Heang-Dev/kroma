2b:T15ba,<p>Long work, like a download, a translation, a voice-over, an export or a whole batch, can take minutes. Kroma tells you when each one finishes or fails, so you can work on something else, or in another app, in the meantime.</p>
<h2 id="the-bell"><a class="anchor" href="#the-bell" aria-hidden="true">#</a>The bell</h2><p>The bell in the title bar, after <strong>New</strong>, <strong>Open</strong> and <strong>Save</strong>, keeps everything that finished or failed: downloads, subtitles, translations, voice-overs, exports, posts, stories, the voice engine and updates. A number on it counts the new ones.</p>
<p>Click it to open the list, newest first. Each entry has:</p>
<ul>
<li>a green tick (done), a red cross (failed) or an info mark,</li>
<li>the title and, under it, the reason or the detail (you can select and copy it),</li>
<li>how long ago it happened,</li>
<li>a button when there&#39;s something to do next: <strong>Retry</strong>, <strong>Open project</strong>, <strong>Show in Finder</strong> (<strong>Show in folder</strong> on Windows and Linux), <strong>Open Downloads</strong>, <strong>Open Batch</strong>, <strong>Open Story</strong>, <strong>Open Settings</strong>, or <strong>Restart</strong> for an update that&#39;s ready.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Mark all read</strong> clears the number; <strong>Clear</strong> empties the list. When there&#39;s nothing, it says <em>You&#39;re all caught up</em>.</p>
<p>The bell keeps the last 100 notifications, and they&#39;re still there after a restart. <strong>Retry</strong> buttons only work until Kroma restarts; after that, start the task again from its panel.</p>

<h3 id="what-to-notify-about"><a class="anchor" href="#what-to-notify-about" aria-hidden="true">#</a>What to notify about</h3><p>A switch for each kind of work: <strong>Downloads</strong>, <strong>Subtitles &amp; translation</strong>, <strong>Voices &amp; stories</strong>, <strong>Export &amp; posting</strong> and <strong>Updates</strong>. Switched off, that kind gets no system notification and no notice in Kroma when it worked. Failures still show, and everything is still kept in the bell.</p>
<h3 id="history"><a class="anchor" href="#history" aria-hidden="true">#</a>History</h3><p><strong>Notification history</strong> shows how many notifications are in the bell. <strong>Clear history</strong> empties it.</p>
<h2 id="not-seeing-them"><a class="anchor" href="#not-seeing-them" aria-hidden="true">#</a>Not seeing them?</h2><p>Your computer decides whether Kroma may show notifications, and whether they make a sound. If <strong>Send test</strong> shows nothing outside Kroma:</p>
<ol>
<li>Open <strong>Settings → Notifications → Not seeing them?</strong> and press <strong>Open</strong>. It opens your system&#39;s notification settings.</li>
<li>On a Mac: <strong>System Settings → Notifications → Kroma Studio</strong>, turn on <strong>Allow notifications</strong> and <strong>Play sound for notifications</strong>. On Windows and Linux, allow notifications from Kroma Studio.</li>
