01 / How it works

From a link to a finished post, step by step.

The status bar along the bottom of Kroma shows what's done and what's next. For a whole series, Batch runs every step on every episode for you.

  1. 1

    Paste a link

    One video, or a whole series, playlist or channel from YouTube, Bilibili, Douyin, Youku, iQiyi, TikTok and 1,000+ sites. Each episode becomes its own project.

  2. 2

    Get the subtitles

    Uses the site's own subtitles, or cloud speech-to-text: a 5-minute video in seconds.

  3. 3

    Translate

    Natural, spoken Khmer, short enough to read and to say in time, with one spelling for every name across the series. Thai, Vietnamese, Lao, English, Indonesian and Malay too.

  4. 4

    Characters & voices

    Kroma finds who says each line. Give the narrator, the king and the hero each their own voice.

  5. 5

    Style

    33 subtitle styles, Khmer fonts and animated captions. The original subtitles are found and blurred away automatically.

  6. 6

    Export & post

    16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for TikTok and Reels, with a numbered cover for each episode, then posted everywhere in one click.

02 / Features

Everything between "found a video" and "posted it".

Khmer that sounds like Khmer

Gemini (free), Google Translate (free) or Claude translate the meaning the way people talk, with the right politeness, common spellings for foreign words, and every line kept to the time it's on screen.

  • A series glossary: every name spelled the same in every episode
  • Notes for the translator: who's who, the tone
  • Lines in the wrong script are caught and translated again

Every character gets a voice

A drama has a narrator, a king, a hero and a mother. Kroma reads the dialogue, finds who says each line and suggests a voice for each one. Change any voice, or who says a line, with one click.

  • Find characters automatically, or add them yourself
  • The original voices removed; music and effects stay
  • Clone your own voice by reading a short text in the app

Post everywhere in one click

The AI writes a title, text and hashtags for each platform. Connect YouTube, Facebook and TikTok once, tick where to post, and Kroma exports the right format for each, uploads, and follows each post to the end.

  • YouTube in 16:9, TikTok and Reels in 9:16, from one project
  • Episode covers with the number: ភាគ ៥, EP 5
  • Batch: check a whole series, then post it from Ready to post

04 / Principles

Built for creators, the open way.

01

Open source

MIT licensed, on GitHub. Read the code, change it, build your own version.

02

Light on your computer

Speech-to-text, translation and voices run in the cloud: a 5-minute video is transcribed in about 3 seconds.

03

Free to start

Gemini and Groq have free tiers; Google Translate and the instant voices need no key at all.

04

Your keys, your files

Videos and projects stay on your computer. API keys are encrypted with your system's keychain.

05

Made for Khmer

Correct Khmer line breaking and shaping, common spellings, Khmer voices for every kind of character.

06

Made for series

Download a whole drama, and let Batch subtitle, translate, voice, style and export every episode while you do something else.

07

Every platform

macOS, Windows and Linux; 9:16 for TikTok and Reels, 16:9 for YouTube, 1:1, from the same project.
